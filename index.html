Workout guide

Easy and long runs

  • Conversational pace in Zone 2.
  • Do not chase speed.
  • If heart rate climbs too high, slow down or walk briefly.

Short intervals (weeks 3 and 6)

  • 10 min easy warm-up.
  • 1 min fast, 2 min slow jog, repeat 5 to 6 times.
  • 5 to 10 min cool-down.

Tempo intervals (weeks 5, 7, 9)

  • 10 min warm-up.
  • 5 min comfortably hard, 2 min easy jog, repeat 3 times.
  • 5 to 10 min cool-down.

Rules

  • About 80% of running stays easy.
  • Never double up to make up a missed workout.
  • Tired? Repeat the week instead of progressing.
  • Stop if knee pain worsens, swells or changes your stride.
